Basketball Stars

Head onto the hardwood for 1v1 and 2v2 arcade basketball action! Pull off alley-oops, blocks, dashes, and unstoppable supershots.

TIP Quick controls

Solo: W A S D or Arrows to move (double-tap left/right to dash), X / L to shoot or steal, Z / K for supershot. 2P: P1 uses W A S D + B / V, P2 uses Arrows + L / K.

NOTE Load time

HTML5 assets load directly in your browser. Click inside the court once loaded to capture keyboard input.
