Football Bros

Call big plays, launch deep passes, stiff-arm defenders, and score touchdowns in fast-paced arcade football matches.

TIP Quick controls

Solo: Move with W A S D or Arrows and use Space, G, or X to snap, pass, dive, and tackle. 2P: P1 uses W A S D + G, P2 uses Arrows + L.

NOTE Focus input

Click inside the field once the game boots so keyboard controls respond cleanly during kickoffs and snaps.
