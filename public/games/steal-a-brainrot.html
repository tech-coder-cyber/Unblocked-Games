Steal a Brainrot

Collect absurd meme characters, defend your base with traps and locks, and raid rival plots to snag rare Brainrots back to your vault.

TIP Quick controls

Click inside the arena to lock camera controls. Look with Mouse, move with WASD, jump with Space, Left Click to swing/defend, and press E to buy, lock bases, or steal Brainrots.

NOTE Load time

Initial Unity WebGL download (~7.7 MB) takes a few moments; avoid refreshing while the progress bar completes.
