Basketball Legends

Hit the court in the classic Flash edition of Basketball Legends. Throw down mega dunks, unleash supershots, and battle solo or in 2-player matches.

TIP Quick controls

Solo: W A S D or Arrows to move/jump/block, X or L to shoot/steal, Z or K for supershot. 2P: P1 uses W A S D + B / V, P2 uses Arrows + L / K.

NOTE Classic Flash

This is the classic Flash edition of Basketball Legends powered by Ruffle (rather than the HTML5 2020 edition).
