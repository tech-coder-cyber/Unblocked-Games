Soccer Bros

Fast 1v1 arcade soccer with outrageous special kicks, slide tackles, and unlockable players across tournament and local 2-player modes.

TIP Quick controls

Solo: Move with W A S D or Arrows and kick/tackle with Space, G, X, or L. 2P: P1 uses W A S D + G, P2 uses Arrows + L.

NOTE Focus input

Click inside the pitch once the game boots so keyboard controls respond immediately during kickoff.
